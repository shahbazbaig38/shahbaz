About

I work at the point where environmental research meets software. At Savonia University of Applied Sciences I build forecasting and analytics systems for energy efficiency, IoT sensor networks, and — most recently — applied AI for manufacturing. Before that I taught mathematics and computing, which is probably why I still care more about whether a system is understandable than whether it's clever.

My research runs alongside a PhD in Environmental Science, but the work I enjoy most is getting a model out of a notebook and into something that runs reliably on its own — which is what's pulling me toward data engineering: building the pipelines and infrastructure that applied ML actually depends on.

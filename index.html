About

I work at the intersection where data engineering meets applied AI. At Savonia University of Applied Sciences I build forecasting and analytics systems for energy efficiency, IoT sensor networks, and applied AI for manufacturing. Before that I taught mathematics and computing — which is probably why I still care more about whether a system is understandable than whether it's clever.

The work I enjoy most is getting a model out of a notebook and into something that runs reliably on its own — building the pipelines, ETL workflows, and infrastructure that applied ML actually depends on in production.
